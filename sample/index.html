What arrives by email

The five questions carry the full explanation and distractor-analysis treatment used in the paid tests. Each wrong answer is handled twice: once for the trap it encodes, once for the student who falls into it.

The solve path

For every item, the reasoning that produces the correct answer, written out step by step rather than asserted.

Distractor logic

Each wrong answer is annotated with the specific misconception it targets — scope overreach, direction error, and the rest — because every wrong answer is engineered rather than filler.

Why a real student might pick each wrong answer

A second pass over the same wrong answers, explaining what a student who is otherwise reading carefully would have to believe in order to choose it.

Before you attempt it

If you are scoring below roughly 1400, finish the official material first. College Board publishes eight full-length adaptive practice tests in Bluebook, plus a large official question bank, at no cost. That is the better use of your time until it is exhausted. This is the guidance printed inside the sample pack itself, and this page will not contradict it.

These items are written for the 1500+ band, where the remaining gap is narrow and specific. They are a supplement to official practice, not a replacement for it. Come back when the official tests are done.
