Frequently asked questions

What the material is, how it reaches you, how the scoring works, and what happens if something in it is wrong.

Is this an official College Board product?

No. 1600 SAT Lab is an independent publisher and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or approved by the College Board. Every question we sell is written in-house, and none is adapted from College Board material.

How is the test delivered, and how fast?

Practice Test 1 is delivered as PDF by email through Stripe checkout, within minutes of purchase. You receive three watermarked PDFs: the test booklet, the answer key with full explanations, and the scoring guide.

Are the questions original?

Yes. All 98 questions in Practice Test 1 are original, and none is adapted from College Board material. Every item is also solved from scratch by a reviewer who did not write it, then audited for double-keys, answer-position patterns, cross-module repeats and lexical give-aways before release.

How hard is this compared to the real test?

The difficulty target is the 1500+ band, and the distribution is disclosed rather than asserted: every module publishes its item-difficulty counts in the answer key. The weight sits in the second modules. Math Module 2 is uniformly top-band, 22 items of 22 in Band 7, and Reading and Writing Module 2 is the heaviest Reading and Writing module at 10 of 27 in Band 7. The first modules are deliberately lighter — they are the on-ramp, matching how the real exam's first module is not the hard path. Two labelling systems are in use, which is worth stating plainly: Reading and Writing is graded on a Band 5/6/7 scale, while Math Module 1 uses easy/medium/hard and was not audited on the Band 5/6/7 scale.

How accurate is the score I get?

Scaled scores come from a hard-path raw-to-scaled curve built for this difficulty profile rather than a curve copied from an official test. The scoring guide discloses its own sources of imprecision: equal item weighting, no adaptive routing, and no empirical calibration against a representative student sample. The test is also non-adaptive, whereas the real Digital SAT routes each student to a Module 2 difficulty based on Module 1 performance. Reading and Writing is scored over 54 questions and Math over 44; a 1500 target corresponds to roughly 50 or more of 54 raw in Reading and Writing, and 40 or more of 44 raw in Math. Any scaled score shown is a pilot estimate from a disclosed internal curve, not an official College Board score.

Can I share the PDF with a friend?

No. Each purchase carries a single-user licence, and redistribution is prohibited. Every PDF is watermarked per buyer with name, email, order number and buyer ID, both in the page footer and in the PDF metadata.

What is the refund policy?

Full refund within 7 days of purchase, no questions asked. Email support@1600satlab.com with your order number. Eligibility ends 7 days after the purchase date because the product is a digital file. The full text is on the refund policy page.

Why should I pay when Bluebook is free?

Start with the free official material. The College Board gives away eight full-length adaptive practice tests in Bluebook plus a large official question bank, and a student scoring below 1400 should finish those first. Buy this when the official material is exhausted and the remaining gap sits at the top of the range. What differs here is treatment rather than volume: every multiple-choice item carries an explicit distractor-logic block naming the misconception each wrong answer targets and explaining why a strong student would pick it. This is not a substitute for official practice.
