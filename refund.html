Refund Policy

All sales are final — with a free reissue or refund for confirmed errors within 30 days.

Last updated: June 7, 2026

All sales are final

1600 SAT Lab products are digital materials delivered immediately upon purchase. Because the content is delivered instantly and cannot be returned, all sales are final.

The one exception: a confirmed error

We stand behind the accuracy of our materials. A refund is issued in one case: a documented factual error in a question or answer key is reported within 30 days of purchase and confirmed by 1600 SAT Lab. In that case, you may choose between:

  • A corrected reissue of the affected material, at no charge; or
  • A refund of the purchase price.

How to report an error

Email us at support@1600satlab.com from the address you used at checkout, within 30 days of your purchase, and include:

  1. Your order number or email receipt;
  2. The test, module, and question number; and
  3. A short description of the error.

We'll review it promptly. If confirmed, we'll arrange your corrected reissue or refund. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method through our payment processor, Paddle, and may take a few business days to appear depending on your bank or card issuer.

Why this policy

Because every purchase is delivered instantly as a downloadable, watermarked PDF, we cannot offer change-of-mind or no-questions-asked refunds. To help you buy with confidence, we offer a free 5-question sample pack so you can judge the quality of our questions and explanations before you buy.

Questions

If you have any questions about this policy or your order, email us anytime at support@1600satlab.com.
