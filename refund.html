Refund Policy

A full refund within 7 days, no questions asked.

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Our promise

We want you to feel confident buying from 1600 SAT Lab. If our practice test isn't right for you, we offer a full refund within 7 days of your purchase — no questions asked.

How the 7-day window works

You are eligible for a full refund if you request it within 7 days of your purchase date. Because our products are digital and delivered immediately, refund eligibility ends 7 days after the purchase date. Requests made after the 7-day window has closed are not eligible for a refund.

How to request a refund

Requesting a refund is simple:

  1. Email us at support@1600satlab.com from the address you used at checkout.
  2. Include your order number or the email receipt you received.
  3. That's it — you don't need to explain why.

We'll process approved refunds promptly. The refund is issued to your original payment method through our payment processor, Paddle. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a few business days for the funds to appear.

Digital product note

Our products are digital downloads delivered by email as PDF files. Because the content is delivered instantly and cannot be "returned" in the traditional sense, the 7-day window described above is the basis for all refund eligibility. We trust our customers and keep this policy simple and generous on purpose.

Questions

If you have any questions about a refund or your order, email us anytime at support@1600satlab.com and we'll be glad to help.
