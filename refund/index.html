Refund policy

Full refund within 7 days of purchase, no questions asked.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. The policy

If you are not satisfied with a purchase, email us within 7 days of the purchase date and we will refund it in full. No reason is required and none will be asked for.

Eligibility ends 7 days after the purchase date. This is because the product is a digital file: it is delivered to your inbox within minutes of purchase, and once that window closes there is no way to distinguish a return from having already used the material.

This policy applies to every product we sell, at the same 7-day window, regardless of price.

2. How to request a refund

Email support@1600satlab.com from the address you used at checkout, so we can match the request to the purchase. Include your order number and the product you bought. That is all we need.

3. How a refund is issued

Refunds are issued through Stripe, which processed the original payment, and are returned to the original payment method. We cannot send a refund by any other route, and we cannot redirect it to a different card or account. How long it takes to appear on your statement is set by your bank or card issuer.

Once a refund is issued, your licence to the refunded product ends and you must delete the files. The watermark carried by every delivered PDF — your name, email, order number and buyer ID, in the page footer and in the file's metadata — remains in any copy that exists.

4. Reporting an error in the material

A refund request and an error report are different things. If you find something you believe is factually wrong in a question or in the answer key — a keyed answer that is not correct, a question where more than one choice is defensible under the stated wording, an arithmetic or algebraic mistake, or an explanation that contradicts the passage or figure it refers to — tell us about it at support@1600satlab.com with the test, module and question number. We check every report against the source reasoning and correct confirmed errors for future buyers, independent of whether you are inside the 7-day refund window.

A difficulty label you disagree with is a published editorial judgement, not an error — every module's difficulty counts are disclosed up front so you can check them before buying. A scaled score that differs from an official result is not an error either: our scoring guide is a pilot estimate from a disclosed internal curve, built for a non-adaptive test, and it states its own sources of imprecision.

5. Judge the material before you buy

The free five-question sample pack comes out of the same production pipeline as the paid tests, with the same explanations and the same distractor analysis, and costs nothing. Three Reading and Writing questions and two Math questions, about 10 to 12 minutes of work. It is the fastest way to judge fit before you spend anything.

6. Contact

Refund requests, error reports and delivery problems all go to support@1600satlab.com.

This policy forms part of our terms of service. Nothing on this page removes rights that the mandatory consumer law of your country of residence gives you.
